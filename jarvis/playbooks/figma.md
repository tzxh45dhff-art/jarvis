# Figma (UI design)

- Left sidebar: Layers/Assets. Right sidebar: Design / Prototype / Inspect
  panels. Toolbar at the top (Move `V`, Frame `F`, Shape `R`/`O`/`L`, Pen `P`,
  Text `T`, Hand `H`, Comment `C`).
- Frames are artboards: select the Frame tool and drag, or pick a device
  preset in the right panel. Auto layout: select items > `Shift+A`.
- Components: `Cmd+Option+K` create; instances inherit changes; variants in the
  right panel. Styles and variables live in the Design panel.
- Prototype: Prototype tab > drag the connection handle to another frame >
  choose trigger/animation; present with `Cmd+Option+Enter`.
- Export: select > Design panel > Export > pick format/scale > Export.
- Share: the Share button (top right) controls who can view or edit.
Figma's canvas is custom-drawn, so rely on show_steps with visible panel and
tool names, and describe icon-only tools.
