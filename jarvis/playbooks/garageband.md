# GarageBand / music production

- Tracks stack vertically; the Library (left, `Y`) holds sounds; Smart
  Controls (`B`) at the bottom; Loop browser (`O`) on the right; Editors
  (`E`). Record `R`; play `Space`; metronome `K`; cycle `C`.
- New track: Track > New Track. Software instrument vs audio vs drummer.
- Loops: open the Loop browser, filter by instrument/genre/mood, drag onto the
  timeline; resize by dragging the right edge.
- Export: Share > Export Song to Disk (choose AAC/MP3/AIFF/WAV).
- Files exported from GarageBand can be edited with video_tool (trim, fade,
  extract audio) and convert_file (mp3, m4a, wav).
